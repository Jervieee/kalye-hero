LEVEL 1 · BARANGAY KALYE

Sunny Manila neighborhood! Grab an easy Baril, bounce on jeepneys & stop street snatchers.

📋 CONTROLS & RULES:
  • Forward / Backward Joystick (Left): Slide Forward for Gas, Backward to Slow/Brake (even inch back slightly!).
  • UP / DOWN Bus Stops: Use ▲ UP / ▼ DOWN (or W/S) to enter glowing Top or Bottom EDSA Bus Stops and hold 🛑 STOP!
  • Keep Pasaheros Happy: Don't crash or miss stops (3 😠 = Game Over). Tap 📯 BEEP to nudge traffic & calm pasaheros!
